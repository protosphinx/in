## What is in/acc?

**in/acc** (Indic Accelerationism) is an open movement that seeks to accelerate India's civilizational progress -- sustainably, inclusively, and openly. We believe technology is not just a tool but a fundamental building block for the future, one that must be wielded with wisdom drawn from India's rich heritage and the urgency of its present challenges.

We don't see degrowth and acceleration as opposites. We want to **degrow our impact on ecology while accelerating civilization**. We want to free markets from crony capture, universalize opportunity, and build digital public infrastructure that serves everyone.

## Core Metrics

| Dimension | Metric | Direction |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Accelerate** | GDP Growth | Grow the pie sustainably |
| **Include** | Gini Coefficient | Narrow the gap |
| **Sustain** | Doughnut Metrics | Stay within planetary boundaries |

In an ideal world, these holistic metrics replace the outsized significance currently enjoyed by GDP alone. We want to align national and private capacity building toward all three north-stars simultaneously.

## Goals

- **Improve Governance** -- Mitigate corruption, complexity, and rent-seeking
- **Accelerate Civilization** -- Sustainably, inclusively, openly
- **Build Civility** -- Trust and safety, contract enforcement, timely delivery of justice
- **Universalize Opportunity** -- Open education, digital public infrastructure, entrepreneurial ecosystems

## Key Focus Areas

- **Anti-Corruption Infrastructure** -- Blockchain-backed transparency, AI-driven fraud detection
- **AI for the Judiciary** -- Case prioritization, jurisprudence analysis, digital evidence management
- **Education & Skill Development** -- Democratized learning, AI-personalized pedagogy
- **Smart Infrastructure** -- Intelligent transport, smart tolls, predictive maintenance
- **Healthcare** -- Diagnostic tools, preventive care platforms, closing the infrastructure gap

Read the full **[Manifesto](./MANIFESTO.md)** for the detailed vision, and the **[Roadmap](./ROADMAP.md)** for what's next.

## Intellectual Foundations

in/acc builds on the philosophy and infrastructure of:

- [India Stack](https://indiastack.org/) & Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)
- [Cosmos / Interchain](https://cosmos.network/) -- federated, sovereign, interoperable systems
- [Complexity Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity_economics) -- embracing uncertainty, emergent order
- [Doughnut Economics](https://doughnuteconomics.org/) -- operating within ecological and social boundaries
