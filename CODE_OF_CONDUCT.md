# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

in/acc is a movement built on the belief that India's progress must be inclusive, sustainable, and open. We extend that same principle to how we work together. Everyone who participates in this community -- regardless of background, identity, or experience level -- deserves a respectful and productive environment.

## Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Engage constructively** -- Offer thoughtful critique, propose alternatives, build on others' ideas
- **Be inclusive** -- Welcome diverse perspectives, communicate across disciplines
- **Be honest** -- Cite sources, acknowledge uncertainty, correct your mistakes
- **Be patient** -- Not everyone shares your context; explain rather than dismiss
- **Respect boundaries** -- Accept when someone disagrees or disengages

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory language
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or projects
- Trolling, bad-faith arguments, or deliberate provocation
- Discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, ethnicity, disability, or any other identity

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all in/acc spaces: this repository, issues, pull requests, community channels, and any events or forums organized under the in/acc banner.

## Enforcement

Community maintainers are responsible for upholding these standards. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Report it** -- Contact the maintainers via a private message or by opening a confidential issue
2. **Action will be taken** -- Maintainers will review, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove participants who violate this code
3. **Appeals are welcome** -- If you believe an enforcement action was unjust, you may appeal to the maintainers

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.
