# Annotated Bibliography

> Status: working bibliography. Journal years and core publication details have been checked against publisher, author, NBER, or RePEc pages where possible. Annotations should expand as each source is worked through.

## Learning Externalities and Industrial Policy Theory

### Greenwald and Stiglitz, ["Helping Infant Economies Grow: Foundations of Trade Policies for Developing Countries"](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2F000282806777212206), AER Papers and Proceedings, 2006

Use: welfare-theoretic foundation for infant-industry policy where learning spillovers make the social return to production exceed the private return.

Paper role: derive the subsidy logic behind manufacturing support rather than asserting that manufacturing is special.

### Greenwald and Stiglitz, [*Creating a Learning Society*](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/creating-a-learning-society/9780231152143), Columbia University Press, 2014

Use: broader framework for development as learning accumulation.

Paper role: frame industrial policy as policy for learning, not only output or employment.

### Aghion et al., ["Industrial Policy and Competition"](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fmac.20120103), AEJ: Macroeconomics, 2015

Use: industrial policy can work better when paired with competition rather than protection from competition.

Paper role: justify export discipline and anti-incumbent capture design.

### Murphy, Shleifer, and Vishny, ["Why Is Rent-Seeking So Costly to Growth?"](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v83y1993i2p409-14.html), AER Papers and Proceedings, 1993

Use: formal political-economy intuition that rent-seeking can redirect talent and resources away from productive activity.

Paper role: support the §11 selection-effects model where firms with political access survive over higher-productivity firms.

### Shleifer and Vishny, ["Corruption"](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/108/3/599/1881822), QJE, 1993

Use: corruption can be more distortionary than taxation because secrecy, illegality, and weak agency control change the structure of costs.

Paper role: support the claim that corruption is not just a transfer but a production and selection wedge.

### Liu, ["Industrial Policies in Production Networks"](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/134/4/1883/5549850), QJE, 2019

Use: formal production-network logic for upstream sector targeting. Liu's distortion-centrality result provides a way to think about sector priority beyond political preference.

Paper role: discipline the sectoral chapter and avoid treating all manufacturing sectors as equivalent.

### Itskhoki and Moll, ["Optimal Development Policies with Financial Frictions"](https://www.econometricsociety.org/publications/econometrica/2019/01/01/optimal-development-policies-financial-frictions), Econometrica, 2019

Use: development policy when private firms face financing constraints.

Paper role: connect manufacturing support to capital-market failures rather than treating tax relief as the only instrument.

### Acemoglu, ["Directed Technical Change"](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/69/4/781/1551628), Review of Economic Studies, 2002

Use: formal apparatus for how market size and relative prices shape the direction of innovation.

Paper role: frame strategic manufacturing missions as direction-of-technical-change policy, not only production support.

### Mazzucato, ["Mission-oriented innovation policies: challenges and opportunities"](https://academic.oup.com/icc/article/27/5/803/5127692), Industrial and Corporate Change, 2018

Use: mission-policy framework for targeted technological and industrial challenges.

Paper role: justify narrow strategic-capacity premia while keeping the broad zero-tax instrument rule-based.

## Empirical Industrial Policy

### Lane, ["Manufacturing Revolutions: Industrial Policy and Industrialization in South Korea"](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/3/1683/8152916), QJE, 2025

Use: rigorous evidence on South Korea's HCI drive, using introduction and termination of policy to study effects during and after intervention. Earlier working-paper versions circulated before final QJE publication.

Paper role: main empirical case for persistent capability accumulation from industrial policy.

### Juhasz, ["Temporary Protection and Technology Adoption: Evidence from the Napoleonic Blockade"](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Faer.20151730), AER, 2018

Use: natural-experiment evidence that temporary protection can induce technology adoption and persistent industrial activity under some conditions.

Paper role: support the argument that temporary wedges can create dynamic capability effects, while also emphasizing sunset design.

### Juhasz, Lane, and Rodrik, ["The New Economics of Industrial Policy"](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-economics-081023-024638), Annual Review of Economics, 2024

Use: current synthesis of the new empirical industrial-policy literature. The NBER working paper appeared in 2023; the Annual Review version is 2024.

Paper role: literature map and standards for causal claims.

### Manelici and Pantea, ["Industrial policy at work: Evidence from Romania's income tax break for workers in IT"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0014292121000271), European Economic Review, 2021

Use: recent empirical work on a tax instrument that targeted a capability-building sector and reported firm, sector, and downstream effects.

Paper role: add modern empirical comparators beyond East Asia and make the zero-tax proposal engage evidence on tax relief as industrial policy.

## India-Specific Manufacturing and State Capacity

### Hsieh and Klenow, ["Misallocation and Manufacturing TFP in China and India"](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-lookup/doi/10.1162/qjec.2009.124.4.1403), QJE, 2009

Use: central estimate that reducing misallocation could raise Indian manufacturing TFP by roughly 40-60%.

Paper role: anchor the missing-middle and misallocation chapter.

### Besley and Burgess, ["Can Labor Regulation Hinder Economic Performance? Evidence from India"](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/119/1/91/1876083), QJE, 2004

Use: state-level labor regulation and manufacturing performance.

Paper role: connect labor institutions to the missing middle and formal employment.

### Kelkar and Shah, *In Service of the Republic*, 2019

Use: Indian state-capacity critique and skepticism toward discretionary intervention.

Paper role: strongest India-specific objection; supports low-discretion policy design.

### Pritchett, Woolcock, and Andrews on capability traps

Use: state capability can be mimicked formally without functional execution.

Paper role: dashboard and audit architecture must measure function, not form.

### Pritchett and Sandefur on the Indian state

Use: "flailing state" frame for inconsistent execution.

Paper role: argue why winner-picking is poorly matched to Indian administrative capacity.

### RBI U.K. Sinha Committee Report on MSMEs, 2019

Use: MSME credit gap and institutional finance constraints.

Paper role: capital and working-capital chapter.

## Economic Complexity and Product Space

### Hausmann, Hidalgo et al., [*The Atlas of Economic Complexity*](https://atlas.cid.harvard.edu/), 2014

Use: product-space framework, economic complexity index, relatedness, and diversification.

Paper role: decide what India should plausibly make next.

### Hidalgo, Klinger, Barabasi, and Hausmann, ["The Product Space Conditions the Development of Nations"](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/publications/product-space-conditions-development-nations), Science, 2007

Use: foundational product-space paper.

Paper role: formal basis for sectoral opportunity mapping.

## Premature Deindustrialization

### Rodrik, ["Premature Deindustrialization"](https://drodrik.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/premature-deindustrialization), Journal of Economic Growth, 2016

Use: developing countries are reaching peak industrialization earlier and at lower income levels.

Paper role: analytical frame for urgency and demographic-window argument.

### Diao, McMillan, and Rodrik, ["The Recent Growth Boom in Developing Economies: A Structural-Change Perspective"](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23132), NBER WP 23132, 2017; Palgrave chapter, 2019

Use: recent developing-country growth accelerations often occurred without rapid industrialization, especially outside East Asia.

Paper role: strongest caution against assuming the manufacturing escalator remains available at full historical scale.

## Comparative Industrialization

### Studwell, *How Asia Works*, 2013

Use: land reform, export discipline, and financial repression as the East Asian development triad.

Paper role: popular but powerful framework for discipline mechanisms.

### Wade, *Governing the Market*, 1990

Use: classic account of East Asian state-led market governance.

Paper role: comparative state-capacity context.

### Amsden, *Asia's Next Giant*, 1989

Use: Korea's late industrialization and reciprocal control mechanisms.

Paper role: rent discipline and performance standards.

### Chang, *Kicking Away the Ladder*, 2002

Use: historical critique of developed-country industrialization narratives.

Paper role: background, not core proof.

## Political Economy

### Khan, ["Political Settlements and the Governance of Growth-Enhancing Institutions"](https://eprints.soas.ac.uk/9968/), 2010

Use: productive versus unproductive rents; political settlement determines whether rents build capability.

Paper role: core apparatus for capture chapter.

### North, Wallis, and Weingast, *Violence and Social Orders*, 2009

Use: limited-access orders and rent allocation.

Paper role: political economy of state-business bargains.

### Acemoglu and Robinson, *Why Nations Fail*, 2012

Use: institutional critique of extractive growth bargains.

Paper role: objection/background; should not dominate the paper.

## Trade Theory and Protection

### Corden, ["The Structure of a Tariff System and the Effective Protective Rate"](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:ucp:jpolec:v:74:y:1966:p:221), JPE, 1966

Use: effective rate of protection.

Paper role: replace nominal tariff arguments with value-added protection analysis.

### WTO, [Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures](https://www.wto.org/english/docs_e/legal_e/scm_e.htm)

Use: legal constraint on subsidies, including revenue foregone and prohibited local-content or export-contingent subsidies.

Paper role: discipline the design of zero-tax eligibility and strategic-capacity premia.

### International Energy Agency, [2022 Increase in Basic Customs Duty on Solar PV Cell and Module Imports](https://www.iea.org/policies/18753-2022-increase-in-basic-custom-duty-on-solar-pv-cell-and-module-imports)

Use: solar module and cell tariff rates for the effective-protection worked example.

Paper role: convert §16 from named trade theory into applied tariff arithmetic.

## Data and Institutional Benchmarks

### World Bank, [World Development Indicators: Manufacturing, Value Added (% of GDP), `NV.IND.MANF.ZS`](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NV.IND.MANF.ZS)

Use: India and Korea manufacturing-share comparisons.

Paper role: empirical anchor for the premature-deindustrialization chapter.

### World Bank Enterprise Surveys, [India 2022 Country Profile](https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/content/dam/enterprisesurveys/documents/country/India-2022.pdf) and [India 2022 Microdata](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/6500)

Use: bribery incidence, transaction-level gift expectations, and raw informal-payments variables.

Paper role: empirical anchor for the corruption-as-manufacturing-tax model.

### World Bank, [Doing Business 2020 India Press Release](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2019/10/24/doing-business-india-top-10-improver-business-climate-ranking)

Use: enforcing-contracts rank and commercial-dispute duration benchmark.

Paper role: empirical anchor for the law, order, and contract-enforcement chapter.
